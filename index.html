The Origin File

"I was just walking across the street. This guy stops, gets out of his car, gives me his card. I thought about it for about two weeks. Then I made the mistake of saying yes."
1993
Born Sarah Joe Chamoun · Beirut, Lebanon
Born into a Lebanese Maronite Catholic family during an era of conflict. French private school education. Arabic at home. Two languages, two worlds — a tension that defines everything that follows.
2001
Family arrives in Montgomery County, Maryland
Age eight. Post-9/11 America. The darkest girl in school, wearing goth clothes, playing lacrosse. Bullied. The outsider. Massanutten Military Academy follows — structure, discipline, survival.
2013
BA in History · University of Texas at El Paso
Works as a bartender, a model, a briefcase girl on a Spanish-language game show to fund it. Graduates. The degree is real. The history degree is not an accident — she studies power, narrative, and who controls it.
2014
3 months · Bang Bros · The hijab scene · The end
October 2014. A producer on a street. Three months. The hijab scene with Julianna Vega goes viral. Within weeks: the most-searched performer on Pornhub globally. ISIS death threats with a digitally manipulated beheading image. Parents publicly disown her. She fires back at Lebanese media within hours. Exits the industry.
2015
#1 on Pornhub · $12,000 · Videos that won't come down
Voted #1 most-searched performer — a year after she stopped. Claimed $12,000 total earned. Bang Bros disputes this, claiming $178,000+ across all companies. She has no residuals. The videos keep circulating. The money keeps flowing — to everyone except her.
2015–19
Paralegal · Bookkeeper · Rebuilding from nothing
Leaves the spotlight entirely. Works as a paralegal and bookkeeper in Miami while the internet debates her identity without her consent. She is doing paperwork in an office while becoming one of the most searched names in internet history.
2023
Vogue India · KNWLS runway · Sheytan · Oxford Union
The reinvention is not sudden — it has been a decade of deliberate building. In 2023 it all becomes undeniable simultaneously. Fashion. Jewelry. Academia. Media. The story is now hers to tell.

$12,000.

Her claim: total earned in 3 months · Bang Bros counter-claim: $178,000+ across all companies · Her royalties since: $0 · Videos still online: yes · Years fighting removal: 10+

Hear her tell it →
The $12,000 Controversy
She received approximately $12,000 directly from Bang Bros. Bang Bros claims they paid $178,000+ total across all companies she worked with. Both figures may be accurate simultaneously — she received $12K from one company. The broader ecosystem's earnings from her content: estimated in the millions, over a decade, continuing today. Her residuals: zero.
The Petition — 1.8M Signatures
Over 1.8 million people signed a petition calling for the removal of her videos from major platforms. This is not a fringe effort — it is a mass civil rights moment for performer digital rights. The platforms have largely declined to act. The videos remain.
#1 in 2015. #1 Again in 2018.
Voted the most-searched performer on Pornhub in 2015 — a year after leaving. Most-searched again in 2018 — three years after leaving. xHamster reported her as most-searched of 2016. The system generates value from her image indefinitely. She generates nothing from that value.
What she has said about it
"I can't even mentally get past somebody wanting to be with me for the novelty. So I'd much rather just be alone." — The Adam Friedland Show, 2026. The personal cost is not abstract. It is a decade of isolation, distrust, and rebuilding.

Sheytan.
Arabic for "devil" · Founded 2023 · Co-created with Sara Burn (ex-Virgil Abloh / YEEZY) · Sold out in 24 hours

The name is the statement. Sheytan — the Arabic word for devil — worn by a Lebanese immigrant woman at Paris Fashion Week, at the Institut du Monde Arabe, in collaboration with a Palestinian label. Every piece of Sheytan jewelry is a provocation with perfect context.

Sheytan World → Collaboration Enquiry
The Name Is the Manifesto

In Arabic, Sheytan means devil. For a Lebanese Maronite Catholic immigrant who has spent ten years being demonised by media, governments, religious institutions and the internet simultaneously — the name is not a provocation. It is an autobiography.

Sara Burn — Former Virgil Abloh / YEEZY

Co-founded with Sara Burn, whose CV includes collaborations with Virgil Abloh and YEEZY. This is not a celebrity vanity project. It is a properly architected label with a co-founder whose credentials are in the serious end of fashion design.

Trashy Clothing × Sheytan · AW26 · Paris

March 2026. Institut du Monde Arabe, Paris. The first-ever Trashy Clothing runway — a Palestinian label. Sheytan provides the jewelry: sword-inspired gold body pieces. "Sword forms repeat across the body, joining as one. Structure and protection." The collaboration is explicitly political, worn explicitly on purpose, in the most precise venue possible.

Sold Out in 24 Hours

The first Sheytan drop sold out within 24 hours of launch in June 2023. The audience was already there — 65 million people across platforms, waiting for something to spend on. The jewelry is the first owned product of the brand. It will not be the last.

Aries — "She Is My Muse"

Aries founder Sofia Prantera on working with her: "I felt immediately inspired by Mia's punk attitude. She is strong but extremely humble. Under her glossy appearance there is a tough young girl who has had to fight her way through so much adversity. I wanted to capture her wild spirit — raw and unpolished." The result: a full campaign and a photo book titled "Mia by Aries."

🇱🇧
Lebanon & Palestine

Lebanese National Anthem tattooed in Arabic. Lebanese Forces Cross tattooed. Lost brand deals after Oct 7th post. Has never walked back her position. The identity is not a performance — it is in her skin. When Beirut exploded in August 2020, she auctioned her iconic glasses for relief funds.

⚖️
Digital Performer Rights

1.8M petition signatures. A decade of advocacy for removal of content filmed without full informed consent. Her case has become the defining example of how the adult entertainment industry's contracts exploit performers — particularly young, financially precarious ones. She did not choose to make this her platform. It chose her.

🗣️
Sex Worker Rights

Has consistently advocated for the rights of sex workers since her exit from the industry — not despite having been one, but because of it. The distinction between exploitation and consent. The distinction between what the industry does to people versus what people choose to do.

🧠
Mental Health & Therapy

Openly discusses therapy and mental health. "I've been in two long-term relationships in the last ten years and that's it. I'd much rather just be alone." The isolation caused by her story is not something she conceals — she names it, documents it, and refuses to perform recovery she hasn't made.
